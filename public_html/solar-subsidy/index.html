CAPACITY SLABS

Benchmark subsidy by system size

Subsidy structures are typically organized by kilowatt capacity. Standard residential systems up to 3 kW and larger systems follow different calculation bands.

Tier 1

Up to 3 kW

Standard residential capacity band. Benchmark subsidy is calculated per kilowatt within published limits set by the applicable scheme: ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW, and ₹78,000 for 3 kW.

Tier 2

Above 3 kW

Larger residential and organizational systems. Subsidy calculation caps at ₹78,000 for individual residential meters, while residential welfare associations (RWA) and group housings enjoy up to 500 kW dedicated slabs.

Calculation basis

Calculation basis

Subsidy is generally based on the installed kilowatt capacity and the rules of the central or state scheme in force at the time of application. Released via DBT upon discom inspection.

DISCOM PROCESS

From feasibility review to sign-off

Connecting a rooftop system to the grid follows a sequence of discom approvals, net meter installation, and commissioning. Each stage must be completed before the next.

01

Feasibility review

The discom reviews the technical feasibility of connecting the proposed system to the local network before any installation proceeds.

02

Net meter installation

A bi-directional net meter records power exported to the grid and power drawn from it, once the discom approves the connection.

03

Commissioning sign-off

After inspection and commissioning, the discom issues sign-off. Verification milestones on the state portal are tracked through this final stage.

04

Subsidy Disbursal

Central government processes the DBT payment directly to your bank account within standard portal processing cycles.
