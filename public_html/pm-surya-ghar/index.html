PM Surya Ghar rooftop solar scheme

A national rooftop solar scheme for eligible residential homeowners. Review the core criteria before you apply. Eligible residential rooftops, sanctioned load guidelines, and a clear route from portal registration to subsidy release.

APPLICATION ROADMAP

From portal to subsidy release

The official route runs through the national portal, a registered vendor, discom inspection, and subsidy release after commissioning.

01

Portal Registration

Homeowner registers on the National Portal (pmsuryaghar.gov.in) with mobile number and electricity consumer number.

02

Vendor Agreement

Select Pranavi Renewables as your registered vendor. Feasibility scan and system layout are submitted.

03

DISCOM Inspection

Installation is carried out. DISCOM conducts site verification and installs the bi-directional net meter.

04

DBT Subsidy Release

Commissioning certificate is issued. Central subsidy is directly transferred to the applicant's bank account.

ELIGIBILITY

Technical rooftop prerequisites

Rooftop capacity limits, shadow-free space, and meter configuration determine how a system is sized for an eligible home.

  • ✓ Shadow-free roof: Minimum 100 sq.ft of unshaded area per 1 kW of installed solar panels.
  • ✓ Sanctioned Load: System capacity is generally matched up to the sanctioned load of your electricity connection.
  • ✓ Grid Availability: Single phase (up to 3kW/5kW) or three-phase line required for net-meter synchronization.
CHECKLIST

Required consumer documents

Prepare identity proof, property ownership record, electricity bills, and bank details before registering on the national portal.

  • 1. Identity Proof: Aadhaar Card of the electricity account holder.
  • 2. Latest Electricity Bill: Recent bill (within last 2 months) reflecting consumer number and sanctioned load.
  • 3. Bank Account Passbook / Cancelled Cheque: In the applicant's name for direct DBT subsidy deposit.
  • 4. Property Ownership Document: Sale deed / house tax receipt matching applicant details.

Assistance with registration and documentation is available through the Contact Us page.
