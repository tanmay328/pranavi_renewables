HOW WE WORK

Compliance, mounting safety, and configuration

Every installation follows electrical compliance requirements, verified mounting hardware, and documented inverter configuration. Each step is checked before a system is energised.

Our engineers confirm wiring and protection against applicable electrical codes, check that racking and fixings suit the roof structure, and configure inverters to the chosen array. Clear documentation is handed over at commissioning so property owners know how their system is set up.

Technical Checklist on Every Site:

  • Wind Load Calculation: Heavy-gauge anodized aluminum rails designed for local wind velocity.
  • Waterproofing Integrity: Chemical anchoring and UV-resistant EPDM flashing on all roof penetrations.
  • Electrical Isolation: Dedicated DC surge protection devices (SPD) and isolators prior to inverter inputs.
  • Earthing & Grounding: Dual-copper chemical earthing pits for lightning arrestors and equipment enclosures.
