ABOUT PRANAVI RENEWABLES

Solar energy is our only focus

Pranavi Renewables works on photovoltaic energy alone. We do not split our attention across general consulting or broader sustainability services. Every assessment, design and installation begins with the solar question in front of us: what this property can realistically generate and how the system should be built around it.

Our Three Principles Get in Touch
OUR COMMITMENT

Transparent assessment and measured performance specifications

Our approach is practical. We look at roof structure, orientation, shading and electrical capacity before recommending a system, so that a property is neither undersized nor oversized for its real energy needs.

We state system sizing, component standards and expected output in plain terms, based on the property we survey. Recommendations are built on documented measurements, not sweeping promises about savings or future returns.

HOW WE WORK

Three operating principles

Each installation follows the same discipline, from the first site visit to long-term service.

Design

Site-specific design

Every system is sized to the roof, orientation and shading of the actual property, supported by a documented energy audit.

Safety

Electrical safety first

We use standard-certified solar components and carry out all electrical work to applicable code requirements.

Care

Long-term serviceability

Systems are installed so that inspection, cleaning and component replacement remain straightforward over the life of the array.
